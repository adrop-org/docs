# R-03: Can Adrop pay users inside mobile and Unity apps, and should price depend on targeting?

Date opened: 2026-10-06. Status: Open
Feeds: `05-business-plan.md` §3, §6; later ADR on identity tiers

## Questions
1. W3AP ships Unity, iOS and Android. Do Apple, Google and the game stores allow an app to pay
   users crypto (USDC) for watching ads? Where can a Unity integration of Adrop actually run?
2. Should there be a cheaper tier? First draft said "unverified viewers"; the owner corrected it
   (2026-10-06): every viewer is verified by some proof, the cheap tier is *untargeted* reach.

## Findings 1: platform rules (checked 2026-10-06)
| Platform | Pay users crypto for watching ads | Rule | Consequence for Adrop |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple App Store (iOS) | **No** | 3.1.5(v): "Cryptocurrency apps may not offer currency for completing tasks, such as downloading other apps, encouraging other users to download, posting to social networks, etc." Also 3.2.2: apps may not require users to watch videos or tap ads "to receive monetary or other compensation" (item numbering shifts; verify) | iOS native is closed to USDC payouts. iOS users can still earn via a web page (Safari / PWA) or a wallet app's in-app browser. No native iOS SDK in the plan |
| Google Play (Android) | **Conditionally yes** | Blockchain-based content policy: apps may let users earn tokenised digital assets if declared in the Financial features form, labelled in the listing, and the app does not "promote or glamorize any potential earning". AdMob separately forbids cash/crypto rewards, but only for Google-served ads; Adrop's ads are not Google-served | Android Unity / native SDK is viable with the declaration, no "earn money" marketing, and no AdMob mediation of Adrop ads. Risk: policy enforcement is uneven (Google removed "deceptive" crypto reward apps) |
| Solana dApp Store (Seeker, Android APK) | **Yes** | permissionless, fee-free, built for crypto categories; 1,561 apps 2026-09 | first and cleanest channel; any Android/Unity build also ships here |
| Steam | **No** | Valve bans games that "issue or allow exchange of cryptocurrencies or NFTs" (2021, still in force) | no PC games via Steam |
| Epic Games Store | **Yes** | allows blockchain games with legal compliance, disclosure, age rating; crypto not through Epic's payment service | PC/console Unity games with Adrop can ship on Epic |
| Web, PWA, wallet in-app browsers | **Yes** | none | today's SDK; also the iOS route |
| Telegram mini apps (TON) | **Yes** | Adsgram already pays developers in TON | later, no personhood proof there |

How W3AP does it: its own app ViewFi runs as a web login, not a listed store app, and pays
$0.01–0.02 per ad in "ViewFi P" (its site, 2026-10-06, "7,300+ community members"). The Unity
SDK pays in BWP / the game's token; where those games are distributed is not disclosed. So the
"iOS and Android" in W3AP's materials is React Native support, not evidence of App Store approval.

Answer to question 1: Adrop with Unity is possible and worth doing, but on **Android** (Google
Play with the declaration, Solana dApp Store without conditions), **Epic** and **web**. Not on
iOS natively, not on Steam. That removes roughly half of mobile game revenue (iOS) from the
native plan; iOS players reach Adrop through the web SDK in a browser or wallet.

## Findings 2: targeting tiers, not verification tiers
(Wallet-list wording here superseded by ADR-0004, 2026-10-08: advertisers may bring their own list; Adrop never exports one.)
Every Adrop viewer is a verified human (one personhood proof, one identity; ADR-0002). What
varies is targeting. Three tiers, one per campaign, price set by the advertiser:

| Tier | Audience | Suggested price / view | Freq cap / campaign | Comparable today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Untargeted | every registered identity | $0.10–0.25 | 3 a day | rewarded video pays the app $0.01–0.02; ViewFi pays the user $0.01–0.02 |
| Targeted | a behaviour segment, e.g. `dex_swap_30d` | $0.50–1.00 | 1 a day | wallet-targeted display CPA $1.86–3.12 (HypeLab) |
| High-intent | narrow segment, e.g. >$10k DeFi volume 30d, NFT collector | $1–5 | 1 a day | quest completion $5–25; DeFi CAC $85 |

Why not a wallet-only "unverified" tier: a wallet costs nothing, so a farm drains any campaign that
pays it, and the fee payer would be paying their fees. It is exactly the fraud the personhood gate
removes. Cheap reach is sold as untargeted verified humans instead; a weaker proof (device
attestation) can later be a lower *proof* tier, but still a proof.

Design: already supported. A campaign with `tags: []` snapshots every registered identity into its
Merkle root; the advertiser picks the price. Server, funding script (`TAGS='[]'`) and the demo
advertiser form expose it (2026-10-06). No program change.

## Findings 3: what untargeted campaigns add to the numbers
See `05-business-plan.md` §7.7 (generated by `research/model.py`). Same humans, two extra cheap
claims a day. Realistic: $876k protocol revenue on $4.38M ad spend, 17% from untargeted.
Best: 8% of revenue. Untargeted is filler for hosts and a cheap entry for advertisers; targeted
is the product.

## Recommendation
1. Unity: yes, as the year-3 step in the plan, Android + Solana dApp Store + Epic + web. State
   plainly that iOS native and Steam are closed to any crypto-paying ad SDK, W3AP's included.
2. Tiers by targeting, every viewer verified. Untargeted campaigns exist today (`tags: []`); the
   demo keeps the targeted campaign as its main beat. Pitch sentence: "untargeted verified humans
   at $0.10, targeted at $0.50–1, same SDK, no bots".
3. Floors: untargeted ≥ $0.10, targeted ≥ $0.25 (R-01 host floor), viewer share ≥ 50%.
What would change the answer: Google tightening the blockchain policy for earn-type apps; Apple
opening 3.1.5(v); advertisers refusing untargeted inventory even at $0.10.
